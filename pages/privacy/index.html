Website privacy notice · Updated 15 October 2026

This website

This version of the website does not include analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies set by its own code. The workflow demonstration uses a fixed synthetic example and does not upload a dataset.

Contact messages

The contact form prepares your name, email, and message in your browser. Selecting Send message opens WhatsApp with those details in a prefilled message; WhatsApp receives the information when the link opens. You confirm sending inside WhatsApp. The separate WhatsApp Message link opens a chat without the form contents. This website does not store form submissions. WhatsApp processes information under its own privacy policy.

Downloads and external links

The Free edition download opens Google Drive. GitHub and Google may process technical information under their own policies when you use their services. This notice describes the website, not the desktop app’s data handling.

License activation

License activation is not enabled. The form does not transmit your name, email, or code while the service is disconnected. Before activation launches, this notice will be updated to describe its operator, processing, retention, and contact details.

Questions

Use the contact page to prepare a privacy enquiry. A direct contact channel will be published when available.
